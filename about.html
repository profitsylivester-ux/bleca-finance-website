About Us

Stewards of BLECA's resources.

The Finance & Accounting Department is the financial backbone of BLECA SmartLabs. We exist to ensure that every shilling the organization receives and spends is properly recorded, approved, and reported.

Our work covers cash and bank management, budgeting, invoicing, payments, document control, and financial reporting. We serve the CEO, the team, our partners, and every funder who trusts BLECA with their resources.

We believe that clear numbers build confidence — for the team today, and for the investors and partners who will carry BLECA into the future.

Our mandate is simple: every shilling must have a purpose, an owner, evidence, an appropriate approval, and a permanent traceable record.

Integrity

Every transaction has a purpose, an owner, and evidence.

Transparency

Anyone authorized can see where the money came from and where it went.

Discipline

We follow process. We document. We do not cut corners.

Stewardship

We treat every shilling as if it belongs to the mission.
